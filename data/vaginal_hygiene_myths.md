Q: whats tss and should i actually be scared of it
A: TSS (toxic shock syndrome) is a rare but serious infection linked to leaving a tampon in too long. If any of that happens while you have a tampon in, take it out and get help immediately. Changing every 4-8 hours keeps the risk very low.
